# Sources and selection record

48 selected stamp entries; this is not a total of everything removed from India. Some entries represent pairs or groups. Selection balances major royal treasures, resistance, craftsmanship, written heritage and documentary evidence. This is not a monetary ranking.

Dates and terms such as reported, attributed, likely, ceded and dispersed preserve differences in evidence. Historical India includes territories beyond present-day borders. Holdings are the latest identified custodian, not confirmation of public display. Private locations may remain unknown.

## Stamp entries

1. **Tipu’s Tiger** — Taken 1799 · Mysore; V&A · London. [Record](https://www.vam.ac.uk/articles/tipus-tiger). The South Kensington Museum was renamed the Victoria and Albert Museum. The journey shows selected documented stages, not every building move.
2. **Bedchamber Sword** — Taken 1799 · Mysore; Private collection · buyer undisclosed. [Record](https://bonhams.shorthandstories.com/a-closer-look-tipu-sword-april-2023/). Publicly documented sale is not proof of present physical location. Intermediate private ownership is described in the auction provenance. Auction provenance records Baird family descent and a 2003 sale before the 2023 auction. The journey cards omit that intermediate sale.
3. **Throne Tiger Head** — Taken 1799 · Mysore; Royal Collection · United Kingdom. [Record](https://www.rct.uk/collection/stories/grand-vestibule-the-british-monarchy-and-the-world/tigers-head). Associated with the Grand Vestibule at Windsor Castle. A collection location does not guarantee public access on a particular day.
4. **Throne Finial** — Taken 1799 · Mysore; Powis Castle · Wales. [Record](https://www.nationaltrustcollections.org.uk/object/1180713). The detailed accession record identifies Richard Wellesley. A new display at Powis Castle opened in February 2026.
5. **Koh-i-Noor** — Ceded 1849 · Punjab; Crown Jewels · Tower of London. [Record](https://www.hrp.org.uk/tower-of-london/crown-jewels/). Image: public-domain 1851 engraving, not a photograph of the current cut. Lahore is now in Pakistan; Punjab was part of historical India.
6. **Ranjit Singh’s Throne** — Taken 1849 · Punjab; V&A · London. [Record](https://api.vam.ac.uk/v2/museumobject/O18891). Current catalogue gives about 1805–1810; older labels differ. Official structured record: https://api.vam.ac.uk/v2/museumobject/O18891. Lahore is now in Pakistan.
7. **Huma Bird** — Taken 1799 · Mysore; Royal Collection · United Kingdom. [Record](https://www.rct.uk/collection/stories/wunderkammer-cabinet-of-curiosities/huma-bird-bird-of-paradise). Display status is not confirmed. Photograph reproduced by thetipusultan.com from Royal Collection imagery; no open reuse licence confirmed.
8. **Royal Tent Panels** — After 1799 · Mysore; Powis Castle · Wales. [Record](https://www.nationaltrustcollections.org.uk/object/1180731.2). Attribution to Tipu’s use is qualified by the collection. The photograph shows the museum installation of the panels.
9. **Tipu’s War Turban** — Taken 1799 · Mysore; National Army Museum · London. [Record](https://www.nam.ac.uk/explore/tipu-sultans-war-turban). The museum article identifies the Global Role gallery display.
10. **Tipu’s Amulet Case** — Taken 1799 · Mysore; National Museums Scotland · Edinburgh. [Record](https://files.nms.ac.uk/production/Documents/Research/Baggage-and-Belonging-Catalogues/national-museums-scotland-department-of-scottish-history-archaeology-hr.pdf?dm=1720792603#page=19). The removal account is attributed to the museum catalogue, not treated as independently witnessed fact. Exact intervening movements and current display are not documented here.
11. **Jewelled Khanjar** — Taken 1799 · Mysore; National Army Museum · London. [Record](https://www.nam.ac.uk/explore/tipu-sultans-war-turban). The separate collection record was intermittently unavailable. The working museum article explicitly includes this dagger. Display status is not confirmed.

42. **Mughal Throne Chairs** — Dispersed 1857 · Delhi; Royal Collection · United Kingdom. Royal Collection crown history records two throne chairs bought from Tytler after Delhi conquest. Counted as one group.
43. **Sher Singh’s Girdle** — Taken 1849 · Punjab; Royal Collection · United Kingdom. [Record](https://www.rct.uk/collection/stories/grand-vestibule-the-british-monarchy-and-the-world/emerald-girdle-of-maharaja-sher-singh). Lahore treasury dismantled by the East India Company in 1849; girdle subsequently presented to Victoria.
44. **Nassak Diamond** — Taken 1818 · Maratha treasury; Private collection · location unconfirmed. [Record](https://www.naturaldiamonds.com/historic-diamonds/nassak-diamond/). Surrendered by Bajirao II and sent to Britain as war spoil. The Peshwa had previously removed it from Trimbakeshwar; British troops did not directly take it from the shrine.
45. **French Royal Bust** — Taken 1799 · Mysore; V&A · London. [Record](https://www.vam.ac.uk/articles/the-tiger-and-the-queen-a-bust-of-marie-antoinette-from-the-court-of-tipu-sultan). Gift to Tipu’s ambassadors; removed during the 1799 looting and acquired by John Adamson Rice. Acquired by the V&A in 1983.
46. **Presentation Sword** — Taken 1799 · Mysore; Private collection · location unconfirmed. [Record](https://www.sothebys.com/en/auctions/ecatalogue/2013/art-imperial-india-l13502/lot.256.html). Eastern European sword kept in Tipu’s treasury, explicitly catalogued as booty from Seringapatam.
47. **Tipu’s Sporting Gun** — Taken 1799 · Mysore; Private collection · location unconfirmed. [Record](https://www.gov.uk/government/news/export-bar-placed-on-rare-18th-century-gun-made-for-the-sultan-of-mysore). Gun made for Tipu; sent to Cornwallis after the fall of Seringapatam. A 2023 export-bar notice does not establish its present location.
48. **Tipu’s Book of Kings** — Taken 1799 · Mysore; University Library · Cambridge. [Record](https://specialcollections-blog.lib.cam.ac.uk/?p=20993). Sixteenth-century Iranian manuscript with an Indian binding, appropriated from Tipu’s library and sent to Cambridge by the Company in 1806.
